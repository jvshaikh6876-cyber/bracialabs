Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Bracialabs respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address when you sign in (for example with Google), and optional details you choose to give such as gender.
  • Photos you upload: used only to create the images you ask for.
  • Payment details: handled by our payment partner (such as Razorpay). We receive only a payment confirmation, not your card or UPI details.
  • Basic usage data: such as credits used and simple technical logs, to run and protect the service.

How we use it

  • To create your images and provide the service.
  • To manage your account, credits and payments.
  • To prevent misuse and keep the service safe.
  • To contact you about your account or important updates.

Your photos

Uploaded photos are sent to our AI image provider only to create your image and are not used by us for advertising. We do not sell your photos or personal data.

Who we share data with

Only trusted service providers that help us run the service, such as cloud hosting, our AI image provider, and our payment partner, and only as needed to provide the service or as required by law.

How long we keep data

We keep account and payment records as long as your account is active and as required by law. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data by emailing hello@bracialabs.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.

Children

Our services are for adults (18+). Parents and guardians who upload photos of their own children are responsible for doing so with care.

Grievances

For any privacy concern or complaint, contact our grievance team at hello@bracialabs.com.
