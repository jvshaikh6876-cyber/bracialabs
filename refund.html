Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

In short: credits are digital and delivered instantly, so purchases are generally non-refundable. But if something goes wrong on our side, we will make it right.

When you get a refund or credits back

  • Failed images: if an image fails to generate because of a technical error, the credits are returned to your account automatically.
  • Payment taken but no credits: if you were charged but credits did not appear, email us and we will add the credits or refund the payment in full.
  • Double payment: if you were charged twice for the same purchase, we will refund the extra payment in full.

When refunds are not given

  • Credits already used to create images.
  • Credits that expired after their validity period.
  • Dissatisfaction with the artistic style of an AI result (you can use "Generate again" or try another style).

Cancellation

Credit packs are one-time purchases, not subscriptions, so there is nothing to cancel and you will never be charged automatically.

How to request a refund

Email hello@bracialabs.com within 7 days of payment with your registered email and payment ID. Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.
